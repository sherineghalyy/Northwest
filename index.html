About NorthWest

Engineering and manufacturing for steel and heavy industry.

NorthWest is an engineering and manufacturing company specialized in high-quality industrial solutions for the iron and steel industry and various heavy industries.

With a strong engineering background and decades of hands-on experience, we deliver customized manufacturing, reverse engineering and technical solutions that meet our clients' specific requirements.

NorthWest has its own production facilities, site support staff and installation crew to manage turnkey projects. We were founded as an independent system provider with an in-house engineering department for process and development.
